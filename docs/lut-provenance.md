## Why these

`aces2_p3d65_sdr100` is the shipped default (decision D5 plus the
2026-09-06 decision recorded in `aces-transform-options.md`). The two
Rec.709 variants back the overlay's View transform control for people on
sRGB displays; §4 of that document shows 33³ is adequate for alternates.

Raw / no transform is not a LUT -- it is an identity path in code.
